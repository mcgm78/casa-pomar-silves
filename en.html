THE PROPERTY

More room for life.
More nature around you.

A six-bedroom house surrounded by a productive citrus orchard, on a 12,240 m² plot comprising urban and rural land. Here, the peace of the countryside meets the space for your plans.

In Poço Barreto, in the municipality of Silves, the property combines living accommodation on two floors, areas arranged as apartments, outdoor spaces and a garage. The layout offers different possibilities: bringing the family together, hosting guests independently or exploring potential rental income.

Balconies and terraces extend the home outdoors. The orchard and countryside offer open views and a gentler pace of life.

MAIN HOUSE · TWO FLOORS

Independence for guests.
Space to share.

On the ground floor, the description indicates a layout comprising two-bedroom and one-bedroom apartments. It includes two bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms and an open-plan living room with an equipped kitchen. The one-bedroom apartment has a bedroom, bathroom and kitchenette.

The first floor comprises three bedrooms, a dining room, a kitchen and a full bathroom. The terrace overlooks the orchard and countryside.

MIXED URBAN AND RURAL LAND · CITRUS ORCHARD
